Rules & paytable

Dealer needs A-K or better. Play bet is 2× Ante. Drawing selected cards or buying a sixth card costs 1× Ante. If Dealer has no game, Ante wins 1:1 and Bet is returned. If Dealer qualifies and you win, BET pays by your best five-card hand. Ties push.
